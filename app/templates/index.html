The learning path

Small steps. Real concepts.

Follow the core building blocks in order, then use the manifests to explore each idea hands-on.

STEP 01 01

Pods & configuration

Run your first workload and provide settings with ConfigMaps and Secrets.

pod.yml · configmap.yml · secrets.yml
STEP 02 02

Workload controllers

Explore replicas, self-healing workloads, and node-level scheduling.

replicaset.yml · daemonset.yml
STEP 03 03

Services & networking

Connect workloads through Services and understand how traffic finds Pods.

services.yml · DNS.md
STEP 04 04

Stateful workloads

See how StatefulSets give applications stable identities and storage.

statefulset.yml
Before you begin

Check your cluster context.

Make sure kubectl is pointed at the cluster you intend to use before running any lab commands.

$ kubectl config current-context
